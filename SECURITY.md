# Security policy · 安全政策

**English.** Please do not open public issues for security problems. Report them privately through GitHub: on this repository, open **Security → Report a vulnerability**. Include the affected URL or file, the steps to reproduce, and the impact. We will acknowledge your report and keep you updated.

In scope: the verifier, SDKs and specifications in this repository; the TapeNow workbench at app.tapenow.dev; Gateway sites under tapenow.dev; and any mismatch between [KEYS.md](KEYS.md) and the live key registry.

Out of scope: denial-of-service, automated scanner output without a working proof of concept, social engineering, and websites that customers publish with TapeNow (report those to their owners).

**中文。** 请不要通过公开 Issue 报告安全问题。请在本仓库的 **Security → Report a vulnerability** 中私下提交，写明受影响的网址或文件、复现步骤和影响范围。我们会确认收到，并告知处理进展。

适用范围：本仓库的验签工具、SDK 和格式规范；app.tapenow.dev 上的 TapeNow 工作台；tapenow.dev 下的 Gateway 网站；以及 [KEYS.md](KEYS.md) 与线上公钥注册表不一致的情况。

不在范围内：拒绝服务攻击、没有可行验证的自动扫描结果、社会工程，以及客户用 TapeNow 发布的网站（请联系网站所有者）。
